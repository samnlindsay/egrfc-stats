Data Notes

Data is pulled from three sources and combined to give the most complete picture available.

  • Google Sheets — primary source from 2020/21 onwards, covering all 1st and 2nd XV games:
    • Date, competition, result, captain, and full team sheet
    • MOTM and individual try/conversion/penalty/drop-goal scorers (from 2025/26)
    • When Veo footage is available: set piece (scrums & lineouts) counts for both teams, detailed attacking lineout coding, and 22m entry stats (from 2025/26)
  • Pitchero — team sheets from before 2020/21 and scorer/MOTM data from 2017/18–2024/25.
  • RFU (englandrugby.com) — full league team sheets and results across all clubs for squad-size and player-retention comparisons.
Gaps to be aware of:
  • Player-level data (appearances, scorers, team sheets) only goes back to 2016/17.
  • No data for the Vets or Colts.
  • Pre-2021 team sheets depend on Pitchero logging — anything not recorded there is missing.
  • Set piece stats only exist when Veo footage was available for that game.

Spotted a gap or an error? Let me know — but bear in mind this is a labour of love, not an official club record.
